# Update the staff list

Each staff member has their own entry with a photo and job title. The Staff page
shows the people chosen for it.

## Edit someone's details

1. In the left menu, click **Staff**.
2. Hover over the person's name and click **Edit**.
3. Change their bio in the text box, or their job title in the **position** box
   below it.
4. To change their photo, click the current image in the **Featured Image** box
   (lower right), choose or upload a new photo, and click **Set featured image**.
   A landscape (wide) photo works best.
5. Click **Update**.

<Shot slug="wp-staff-entry" caption="A staff entry in the editor with the position box and the Featured Image box." />

## Add a new staff member

1. Click **Staff**, then **Add New**.
2. Type their name as the title, write a short bio, fill in their position, and
   set a featured image.
3. Click **Publish**.
4. Now add them to the Staff page: go to **Pages**, open the **Staff** page,
   scroll down to the staff list, click **Add Row**, and pick their name.
5. Click **Update**.

<Shot slug="wp-staff-page-rows" caption="The staff list rows on the Staff page with the Add Row button." />

## Someone leaving?

Don't delete their entry. Remove their row from the Staff page (step 4 above, in
reverse) and tell David.
