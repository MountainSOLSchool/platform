# Log in

The website's login page is **not** at the usual WordPress address. This is on
purpose, to keep out bots.

1. Use the login link David gives you. Bookmark it on your computer and phone.
2. Type your username (or email) and password.
3. Click **Log In**.
4. You'll see the **Dashboard**: a dark menu down the left side and a welcome
   area in the middle.

## Tips

- Forgot your password? Click **Lost your password?** under the login box, or
  ask David.
- Don't share your login with anyone. If someone else needs to edit the site,
  ask David to make them their own.
- The website login is separate from your [enrollment portal](/getting-started/admin-access)
  login. Signing in to one doesn't sign you in to the other.
