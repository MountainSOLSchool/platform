# Check your change on a phone

Most families visit on their phones, so check there before and after you
publish.

1. Before you click Update, click **Preview**. Copy the address from the preview
   tab and open it on your phone while logged in. Or simply make your browser
   window narrow on your computer.
2. Look for: words that run off the edge, huge or tiny photos, and buttons that
   are hard to tap.
3. After you click **Update** or **Publish**, open the real page on your phone.
4. Don't see your change? Wait a minute and reload. Still old? Tell David.
