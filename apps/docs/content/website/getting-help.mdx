# Who to ask

Ask **David** at **david@mountainsol.org** for anything not in this guide,
including:

- You can't log in or forgot your password.
- Something looks broken, or you think you made a mistake.
- Changing prices, the top menu, or a page's web address.
- Deleting or archiving pages, posts, events or staff.
- Adding a new person who needs a login.

When you ask, include the page's web address and a screenshot if you can. That
makes it much quicker to help.
