# Add a post or news item

**Posts** are dated news items and announcements. The newest ones show first.

1. In the left menu, click **Posts**, then **Add New**.
2. Type a short, clear title in the top box, for example *"Fall Open House on
   October 12"*.
3. Write your news in the big text box. Keep it short: what, when, where, and
   what to do next.
4. On the right, under **Categories**, tick the one that fits.
5. Optional: add a photo (see [Add or replace a photo](/website/photos)) and set
   a **Featured Image** in the box on the lower right.
6. Click **Preview** to check it.
7. Click **Publish**.

## Tips

- Not ready yet? Click **Save Draft** instead of Publish. It stays hidden until
  you publish it.
- Want it to go out on a set day? Next to **Publish immediately**, click
  **Edit**, pick the date and time, click **OK**, then click **Schedule**.
