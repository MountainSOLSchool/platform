# Add a sponsor logo

1. Ask the sponsor for their logo as a **PNG** or **JPG**. A logo on a clear or
   white background looks best.
2. Go to **Pages** and open the page with the sponsor logos (for example
   Sponsors or Donate).
3. Click where the logo should go, next to the others.
4. Add the logo with **Add Media** (see [Add or replace a photo](/website/photos)).
   Use the sponsor's name as the **Alt Text**.
5. Optional: link the logo to the sponsor's website. Click the logo, click the
   pencil icon, and under **Link To** choose **Custom URL** and paste their web
   address.
6. Click **Update** and [check it on a phone](/website/checking-on-a-phone).
