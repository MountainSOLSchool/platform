# Website basics

This section is for staff who update the public website, **mountainsol.org**.
You don't need to be technical. Each page covers one task, with numbered steps
you can follow one at a time.

If anything looks odd or you get stuck, stop and ask David (see
[Who to ask](/website/getting-help)). You can't break the site by asking.

## Before you start

- The website is built with **WordPress**. The part you work in is called the
  **Dashboard**.
- Nothing you change is seen by visitors until you click **Publish** or
  **Update**.
- Every page and post keeps its old versions, so a mistake can be undone.
- Want to practise first? Ask David for a login to the **practice copy** of the
  site. It looks and works just like the real one, but nobody else sees it.

## Tasks

- [Log in](/website/logging-in): get to the Dashboard.
- [Edit a page](/website/editing-a-page): change the words on About, Summer, Fall, Donate and other pages.
- [Add a post or news item](/website/posts-news): dated news and announcements.
- [Add or replace a photo](/website/photos): including photos of students.
- [Update a class or event listing](/website/class-event-listings): dates, ages and Register links.
- [Fix a link](/website/links): change, add or remove a link.
- [Update the staff list](/website/staff-list): bios, photos and new staff.
- [Check your change on a phone](/website/checking-on-a-phone): most families visit on their phones.
- [Add a sponsor logo](/website/sponsor-logos)
- [What not to touch](/website/what-not-to-touch): areas that are David's job.
- [Who to ask](/website/getting-help)

> The website and the [enrollment portal](/getting-started/admin-access) are two
> separate tools with separate logins. Class sign-ups, payments and rosters live
> in the portal; the website just describes classes and links to the portal.
