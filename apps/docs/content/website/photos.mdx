# Add or replace a photo

Photos straight from your phone are fine. When you upload, the website
automatically:

- turns the photo the right way up,
- shrinks it to a sensible size,
- removes hidden location (GPS) and camera details, and
- saves it in a smaller, faster format.

So you don't need to resize or edit photos first. Upload **JPG** or **PNG**
photos.

## Add a photo to a page or post

1. Open the page or post (see [Edit a page](/website/editing-a-page) or
   [Add a post](/website/posts-news)).
2. Click in the text where you want the photo.
3. Click **Add Media** (just above the text box).
4. Click **Upload Files**, then **Select Files**, and pick your photo. Or drag
   the photo into the window.
5. On the right, fill in **Alt Text**: one short sentence describing the photo
   for people who can't see it, for example *"Students building a shelter in the
   woods"*.
6. Under **Size**, pick **Large** or **Medium**.
7. Click **Insert into page** (or **Insert into post**).
8. Click **Update** (or **Publish**).

## Replace a photo

1. Open the page or post and click the photo you want to replace.
2. Click the small **X** (remove) icon that appears on it. This only takes it off
   the page; it stays in the Media Library.
3. Add the new photo using the steps above.
4. Click **Update**.

<Shot slug="wp-photo-selected" caption="A selected photo in the editor showing the edit and remove icons." />

## Photos of students

- Only use photos of children whose families have agreed to photos online. If
  you're not sure, don't post it and ask first.
- Don't put children's full names in the photo, the caption or the alt text.
