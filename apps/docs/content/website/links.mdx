# Fix a link

## Change where a link goes

1. Open the page or post (see [Edit a page](/website/editing-a-page)).
2. Click on the linked words in the text box.
3. A small box pops up showing the link. Click the **pencil** (edit) icon.
4. Paste the correct web address into the box.
5. Click the **blue arrow** (Apply) button.
6. Click **Update**.

<Shot slug="wp-link-popup" caption="The pop-up link box with the pencil icon." />

## Add a new link

1. Highlight the words you want to turn into a link.
2. Click the **chain** icon in the toolbar (or press **Ctrl+K**, or **Cmd+K** on
   a Mac).
3. Paste the web address, or type a few words to search for one of our own
   pages.
4. Click the **blue arrow** (Apply).
5. Click **Update**.

<Shot slug="wp-insert-link" caption="The toolbar with the chain (insert link) icon." />

## Tips

- Always click the link in Preview to make sure it opens the right page.
- To remove a link but keep the words, click the linked words and then the
  **broken chain** icon.
- A link in the site's top menu is not on a page. Ask David to change those.
