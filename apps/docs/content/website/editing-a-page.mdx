# Edit a page

**Pages** are the parts of the site that stay put, like About, Summer, Fall or
Donate.

1. In the left menu, click **Pages**.
2. Find the page. You can type its name in the **Search Pages** box at the top
   right.
3. Hover over the page name and click **Edit**.
4. Click into the big text box and make your change, just like in a Google Doc.
   Use the **Visual** tab (top right of the text box), not the **Text** tab.
5. Click **Preview** (in the **Publish** box on the right) to see the change in a
   new tab.
6. When it looks right, click **Update**.
7. Click **View Page** at the top to check it on the live site.

## Tips

- Change only what you came to change. Leave the page's **Template** and the
  other boxes on the right as they are.
- Made a mistake after clicking Update? In the **Publish** box, click
  **Revisions** (or **Browse**) to see older versions, then click **Restore This
  Revision** on the good one.
- Before you click Update, [check it on a phone](/website/checking-on-a-phone).
